## Asiabots Digital Human — What technology does it use?

I found the official technical information behind the Digital Human (GAI + Deep Fake) option in your screenshot.

The key finding: Asiabots uses a combination of generative AI, face-swapping, AI lip-sync, speech synthesis, speech recognition, and its proprietary AI Ambassador animation engine.

Its digital human technology is not simply a 3D model or a prerecorded video. It is designed to generate realistic talking-human animations that respond to live conversations.&#x20;

## 2. How its Digital Human system works

Based on Asiabots' published descriptions, the likely real-time processing architecture is:

User speaks

Microphone input

ASR / Speech Recognition

Converts speech into text

Geniichat™ / LLM

Understands the question and generates a response

TTS Engine

Generates voice audio

Animation Engine

Generates facial and body movements

Real-Time Digital Human

Talking face, synchronized audio and animation

Conceptual reconstruction of its published capabilities, not Asiabots' disclosed internal software architecture.
